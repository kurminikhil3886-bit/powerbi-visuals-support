IBCS Variance Table

IBCS-style variance by category: a hierarchy with subtotals, actual bars, Δ bars and Δ% pins, Top N and a sortable table view.

Download .pbiviz All downloads and install steps

Need help? Open an issue. Include the version of the visual, your Power BI Desktop version and a screenshot.

Requirements

  • Power BI Desktop (current release), the Power BI service or Power BI Report Server with custom visuals enabled.
  • One or more category fields and an actual measure.

Setting up

Field wellWhat to add
Category (one or more levels)Required. Several fields become an expandable hierarchy with subtotals.
Actual (AC)Required. Its format string is used for all values.
Previous year (PY)Optional. Grey bar behind the actual; basis of ΔPY and ΔPY%.
Plan / Budget (PL)Optional. Outlined bar; basis of ΔPL and ΔPL%.
Forecast (FC)Optional. Hatched bar; basis of ΔFC and ΔFC%.
Extra tooltip valuesOptional. Shown in the tooltip of single rows.

Troubleshooting

Increases show green for a cost measure

Turn off Higher is better (Format pane › Colors).

Subtotals of a ratio are wrong

Subtotals are sums of the rows. For ratios such as a rate, use a single level or show the ratio in the tooltip.

A large Δ% is cut off with »

Relative variances are clipped at 150% by default. Change Clip relative variance under Columns and layout.

Removing a cross-filter

Click the selected item again, click empty space or press Esc.

Keyboard use

Tab into the rows, Up / Down to move, Left / Right to collapse or expand, Enter or Space to select, Shift+F10 for the context menu and Esc to clear.

Known limitations

  • Up to 30,000 rows are loaded.
  • The interface is in English.

Privacy

The visual has no network access and does not collect, send or store your data. See the privacy policy.
