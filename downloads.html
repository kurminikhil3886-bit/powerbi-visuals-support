Good to know

  • Updates are not automatic for visuals imported from a file. To update, import the newer file into the report; your field wells and formatting are kept.
  • Organization settings: if the import option is missing or the visual does not load, your Power BI administrator may have turned off visuals imported from files. Ask them to allow it or to add the visual to your organization’s visuals.
  • Export to PowerPoint or PDF and email subscriptions show only certified visuals; these visuals are not certified yet.
  • Check the file: the SHA-256 above should match the file you downloaded (Windows: Get-FileHash file.pbiviz).
  • Privacy: the visuals do not collect or send your data. See the privacy policy.
